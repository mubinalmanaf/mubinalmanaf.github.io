The problem

The world got louder.
Stillness got harder to find.

We reach for our phones to feel calm and find more noise. Somewhere in the scroll, the quiet part of us stops being heard.

The turn

Rhumi is that quiet — returned to you each day.

Not another feed. A single, unhurried moment that asks nothing of you but your attention.

The ritual

Three quiet steps, once a day.

No streaks to protect. No feed to finish. Just a moment that's yours.
